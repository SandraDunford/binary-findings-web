Our Remote-First Approach

How Binary Findings works to a remote-first consultancy delivery model.

Last updated: 29 July 2026

1. Purpose

Binary Findings is a remote-first consultancy. We believe modern digital technologies enable exceptional professional services to be delivered without being constrained by geography. Our remote working approach is designed to provide flexible, responsive, sustainable, and secure services while maintaining strong relationships with our clients

2. Our Philosophy

We believe that remote working represents a progressive approach to professional consultancy. By reducing unnecessary travel, embracing secure digital collaboration, and investing in modern technology, we are able to:

  • reduce environmental impact through lower travel-related emissions
  • improve responsiveness to client enquiries and project needs
  • provide specialist expertise regardless of geographical location
  • make more effective use of time and resources
  • support healthier, more flexible ways of working
  • deliver high-quality consultancy without compromising professionalism
Remote working is not a compromise; it is our preferred operating model because it benefits our clients, our consultants, and the wider community.

3. Working with Clients

Most consultancy services are delivered remotely using secure digital communication platforms, including:

  • Microsoft Teams
  • Microsoft 365
  • Secure cloud document sharing
  • Email
  • Telephone
  • Online workshops and presentations
Where appropriate, face-to-face meetings, workshops, or site visits can be arranged by prior agreement.

4. Security

Remote working does not reduce our commitment to information security. Binary Findings employs appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect client information, including secure authentication, encrypted communications where appropriate, and adherence to our Information Security and Data Protection policies.

5. Availability

We aim to provide timely responses during normal business hours. Where project requirements demand it, flexible working arrangements may be agreed with clients to accomodate workshops, deadlines, or different time zones

6. Continuous Improvement

We continually evaluate new technologies and collaborative practices that improve communication, productivity, accesibility, and sustainability while maintaining the highest standards of professional services.
