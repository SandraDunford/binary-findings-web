# Privacy Policy **Last updated:** 24 July 2026

1. Introduction

Binary Finding ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal data when you visit our website or contact us. By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Who We Are

Binary Finding provides specialist data consultancy and analytical services across healthcare and other sectors. **Website:** https://www.binaryfindings.co.uk **Email:** [enquiries@binaryfindings.co.uk](mailto:enquiries@binaryfindings.co.uk)

3. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name
  • Your organisation
  • Your job title
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number
  • Information you provide through enquiry forms
  • Information submitted when requesting a quotation
  • Information provided when booking a consultation
  • Any information included in emails or correspondence with us
We do not intentionally collect special category personal data through our website. Please avoid including sensitive personal information unless specifically requested.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Prepare quotations.
  • Arrange consultations.
  • Communicate regarding our services.
  • Deliver services you have requested.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Maintain records of our business communications.
We do not sell your personal information.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Where UK data protection law applies, we process personal information on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Your consent.
  • Taking steps prior to entering into a contract.
  • Performance of a contract.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Our legitimate interests in operating and improving our business.

6. Information Security

Binary Findings is committed to maintaining appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the information entrusted to us. Access to business systems is protected using Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), strong password management and role-based access controls where appropriate. Our business systems are hosted on Microsoft 365 Business Premium, providing enterprise-grade security services including encrypted email, advanced threat protection, anti-phishing and anti-malware safeguards, and continuous security monitoring. Business data is stored securely within Microsoft SharePoint and OneDrive, where it is encrypted both in transit and at rest. Company devices are protected using Microsoft Defender endpoint security and will utilise BitLocker full-disk encryption following the planned upgrade to Windows 11 Pro. Regular cloud backups, file versioning and secure offline backups using encrypted external storage support data resilience and business continuity. Passwords are generated and securely managed using Bitwarden, and we regularly review our security practices to maintain compliance with applicable data protection legislation and recognised information security principles.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your information may be shared with trusted third-party service providers where necessary to operate, maintain and improve our business and website, including:

  • Microsoft 365 services (including Outlook, Microsoft Forms, Microsoft Bookings, SharePoint and OneDrive)
  • Website hosting and domain service providers
  • Website analytics providers (such as Google Analytics), where you have provided consent for analytics cookies
  • Professional advisers (such as accountants, insurers or legal advisers) where necessary
  • Regulatory bodies or law enforcement where required by law

We only share personal information where it is necessary, proportionate and in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation. Where third-party providers process personal data on our behalf, they do so under appropriate contractual and security obligations.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

  • Request transfer of your information where applicable.

  • Request transfer of your information where applicable.
