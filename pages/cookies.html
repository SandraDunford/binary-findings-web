Cookie Policy

Information about how Binary Finding uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Last updated: 27 July 2026

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Binary Findings ("we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

Cookies help our website function properly, improve your browsing experience and help us understand how visitors use our website.

By continuing to use our website, you agree to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy unless you choose to disable them through your browser or cookie preferences.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, tablet or mobile device when you visit a website.

Cookies allow websites to remember information about your visit, such as your preferred settings, making future visits easier and improving your experience.

Cookies cannot access information stored elsewhere on your device.

3. Cookies We Use

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may use the following categories of cookies.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to operate correctly and cannot normally be disabled.

Examples include cookies that:

  • Remember your cookie preferences.
  • Maintain website security.
  • Support basic website functionality.

Without these cookies, parts of the website may not function correctly.

Analytics Cookies

If enabled, we use analytics services to understand how visitors use our website.

These cookies may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited.
  • Time spent on the website.
  • Browser type.
  • Device type.
  • Approximate location (country or region).
  • Referring website.

This information is anonymised wherever possible and is used solely to improve our website and services.

Functional Cookies

Some features may rely on cookies provided by trusted third-party services.

Examples include embedded services such as:

  • Microsoft Forms.
  • LinkedIn.
  • GitHub.

These providers may place their own cookies when you interact with their services. Please refer to their individual privacy policies for more information.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links or embedded content from third-party providers, including:

  • Microsoft.
  • LinkedIn.
  • GitHub.

These organisations may use cookies independently of Binary Findings.

We do not control these cookies and recommend reviewing the privacy and cookie policies of those providers.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your web browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete cookies.
  • Block specific cookies.
  • Block all cookies.
  • Receive notifications before cookies are stored.

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect how the website functions.

6. Cookie Consent

Where required by UK data protection and privacy laws, we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device.

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your web browser settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legislation, technology or our services.

Any updates will be published on this page together with the revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy or how we use cookies, please contact us.
